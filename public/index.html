Data sources & coverage

Winning numbers: NY Open Data, beginning February 2010. Results refresh automatically on opening and every five minutes while this page is open. Latest numbers and jackpot estimates are checked against Powerball; NY Open Data supplies the historical draw archive. Jackpot claims: Powerball Media Center, archive snapshot from October 19, 2026. Only records with a published Cash or Annuity election count as claimed. The site checks Powerball’s published claim records at each refresh; claims may be reported later than their drawing dates. Saved draws stay in this browser. Verify all results with your state lottery.
