Since 1992, Powerball® has offered players the chance to win life-changing prizes while helping U.S. lotteries raise more than $38 billion for public programs and services. Powerball is home to some of the largest jackpots in lottery history, including the world-record $2.04 billion jackpot won by a single ticket in California on Nov. 7, 2022.

Powerball tickets cost $2 per play and are sold in 45 U.S. states, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands. More than half of all proceeds from Powerball ticket sales remain in the jurisdiction where the ticket is purchased to support local programs and services. Beginning July 21, 2026, Powerball ticket sales will expand to the United Kingdom.

Drawings are held every Monday, Wednesday and Saturday at 10:59 p.m. ET and are broadcast live from the Florida Lottery Draw Studio in Tallahassee. Drawings are also livestreamed on Powerball.com.

To play, select five numbers from 1 to 69 and one Powerball number from 1 to 26. The Powerball jackpot starts at $20 million and grows until it is won. Players can win prizes in nine different ways, with the jackpot awarded for matching all five white balls and the red Powerball.

Jackpot winners may choose to receive their prize as an annuity with 30 increasing payments over 29 years or as a one-time lump-sum payment. Both prize options are before applicable federal and jurisdictional taxes.

The overall odds of winning a Powerball prize are 1 in 24.9. The odds of winning the jackpot are 1 in 292.2 million.

Power Play® and Double Play®

Powerball offers two optional add-on features: Power Play® and Double Play®.

For an additional $1 per play, Power Play multiplies non-jackpot prizes by 2X, 3X, 4X, 5X or 10X. The Match 5 + Power Play prize is always $2 million. The 10X multiplier is available only when the advertised jackpot is $150 million or less.

For an additional $1 per play, Double Play gives players another chance to win a separate drawing using the same numbers played in the Powerball drawing. Double Play features a top cash prize of $10 million and eight additional cash prizes. Double Play drawings are held after each Powerball drawing and are available in participating jurisdictions.

Powerball launched on April 22, 1992, with 15 participating lotteries: Delaware, the District of Columbia, Idaho, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Minnesota, Missouri, Montana, Oregon, Rhode Island, South Dakota, West Virginia and Wisconsin. Today, 48 U.S. lottery jurisdictions offer the game. Beginning July 21, 2026, Powerball ticket sales will expand to the United Kingdom.

Over the years, Powerball has evolved to create larger jackpots and more prize-winning opportunities. The current game matrix (5/69 + 1/26) debuted on Oct. 7, 2015, increasing the Match 4 + Powerball prize from $10,000 to $50,000 and introducing a 10X Power Play multiplier.

In August 2021, Powerball expanded to three weekly drawings by adding a Monday drawing to its existing Wednesday and Saturday schedule. The Double Play® feature also launched, providing players in participating jurisdictions with an additional drawing and a top prize of $10 million.

Powerball became the first multi-jurisdictional lottery game to surpass the $1 billion jackpot mark when three tickets shared a $1.586 billion jackpot in January 2016. The game continues to hold the world record for the largest lottery jackpot ever won: a $2.04 billion prize won by a single ticket in California on Nov. 7, 2022.
